A prose judge now runs between dispatch and the submission plan (Step 3's pipeline gains an item): every validated claim's rendered comment is judged against the vendored plain-prose rubric (verbatim from the reference implementation, plus a per-label loose length tier), and a failing claim's `discussion` is replaced by one constrained rewrite (at most one claim, one line of evidence, at most one question) before the plan composes. Enforcement is structural rather than eval-gated: a claim is never dropped and only its prose is rewritten; a judge error, unparseable verdict, or refused rewrite posts the original text; and the run artifact records four states per claim (skipped/pass/fail/error) in `judge-prose-verdicts.json`, so a broken backend reads as errors, not clean passes. The model call goes through the Agent SDK (the sandbox strips `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and proxy-injects auth, so a raw fetch would fail-open the judge into a silent no-op) and is pinned to the haiku tier. The sub-agent `discussion` field contracts carry the same shape line, so comments are born short and the judge stays a backstop.
