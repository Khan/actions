Repeat reviews post the full surface: the `blocking-only` and `blocking-medium` re-review posting modifiers are removed (PRA-53). They filtered what a reduced-depth repeat review posted inline, built to contain re-review chatter that turned out to be the dead re-review fingerprint (PRA-52) re-running every round at full depth over the whole diff. With the fingerprint working, `scoped` staging already bounds a repeat round to new hunks, so what posts on a re-review is feedback about code the author just pushed, filtered by the same rules as a first review: the non-blocking inline budget (default 3), the nitpick ban, and the confidence floor. A leftover modifier in a consumer's ROUTING file warns and is ignored; the mode still applies. The medium importance tier itself is unchanged (ranking, the changed-lines veto, the COMMENT verdict demotion, and the per-run medium count all stay); only its posting carve-out died with the reduced surface, since there is no reduced surface to punch through. The pr-level collapse bucket the modifiers introduced is gone too: pr-level claims fold into the body verbatim at every depth, and the collapsed section's "Non-blocking observations" title retires with it (the neutral "Lower-confidence observations" title is the only one left).

Expected output-shape effect: on reduced-depth re-reviews, up to `non-blocking-budget` non-blocking comments post inline where blocking-only posted zero and blocking-medium posted only medium ones; the "collapsed into the body (re-review ...)" note line disappears; the depth note and version footer drop the modifier suffix. First reviews and full-depth rounds are unchanged.
